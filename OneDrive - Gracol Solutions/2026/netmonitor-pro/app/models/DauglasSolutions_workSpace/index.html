Our Cyber Security Framework

Comprehensive Protection for Your Business Assets and Data

At Dauglas Solutions, we follow a proven 5-phase approach to protect your business from cyber threats, ensuring confidentiality, integrity, and availability of your critical assets.

1. Assess & Plan

Identify your assets, map threats, and define protection goals. We evaluate systems, networks, data, applications, and users to create a tailored security strategy.

2. Protect

Secure your network with firewalls, VLANs, and VPNs. Implement access controls, MFA, RBAC, and data encryption to harden your infrastructure.

3. Detect

Deploy monitoring tools like SIEM, IDS/IPS, and AI-assisted threat detection. We monitor communications and systems for suspicious activity.

4. Respond

Have an incident response plan ready. We identify, contain, and eradicate threats quickly, notifying stakeholders and escalating through our portal.

5. Recover

Maintain regular backups and test restore processes. We ensure fast recovery, review incidents, and improve policies for ongoing protection.
